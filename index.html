Who This Is For

Three Qualifiers, One Application Gate

Cohort is application-only. We're filtering for operators who can get the most out of a peer room of portfolio builders, and who'll add the most signal to it.

01

Your channel is producing $5K+/month or has crossed monetisation. You've shipped enough video to know what works at single-channel scale. The Cohort is not where you start. It's where you scale what you've already proven.

02

Questions

Before You Apply

The logistics and the qualification questions. Anything not covered here, we cover on the application call.

What's actually included in the seat?

Two days in person at a US venue, including the live channel audit, the 1-on-1 breakout with Matt on Day 2, all sessions, all meals during the event, and the cohort dinner. Plus 90 days of post-event implementation: bi-weekly group calls, direct 1-on-1 access to Matt for in-deal questions, the cohort-only channel for peer feedback, and the implementation calendar locked in. Travel and accommodation are not included, but a hotel block is reserved at a recommended venue within walking distance.

What if I haven't run a monetised channel yet?

Then this isn't the right fit yet. The Cohort is built for operators who already have at least one monetised channel ($5K+/month) and want to scale to a portfolio. If you haven't started, Tube Accelerator is the right starting point. If you've started but haven't hit monetisation or $5K/month, the Cohort would be premature. Apply once you've cleared that gate.

Can I send a partner or co-founder instead of attending myself?

The seat is per operator. If you and a partner co-run the portfolio together, contact us about partner pricing for the second seat at a reduced rate. We'd rather have both decision-makers in the room than have one of you take notes for the other.

How does the 90-day implementation actually work?

Bi-weekly group calls (six total over 90 days) walking through what each operator has shipped against their plan. Direct 1-on-1 access to Matt for in-deal questions throughout the 90 days, with response on a published cadence. The cohort-only channel runs throughout for peer feedback. Implementation is yours to ship; the structure is to keep momentum and unblock decisions when they happen.

What's the application process?

You book a 20-minute application call. We confirm fit (your channel, your portfolio goal, your bandwidth, your timing). If fit is clear and a seat is open, we lock in the seat. If fit is unclear, we either flag the gap to clear before the next intake, or recommend Tube Accelerator as the right starting point. Cohort is application-only; not every applicant gets a seat.

What if I need to defer to a later cohort?

Once a seat is locked and paid, you can defer to the next cohort one time, no questions, no penalty. Beyond one deferral we'd need to discuss whether the current intake is still the right fit. Refunds are not standard once a seat is locked, but we'd much rather have you in the right cohort than the wrong one.
